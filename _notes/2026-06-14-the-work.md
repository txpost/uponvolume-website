Do the work.

Ok, sure. But what is the work?

I want to work hard, but I also want to work smart.

In learning to trade, the work is studying thousands of charts. It's committing thousands of hours of deliberate practice to learn one setup.

That's the edge. That's the hard work others are not willing to do.

Those thousands of hours is the hard work others are not willing to do.

There are no shortcuts.
