I see phrases like do the work, do more, work harder, keep going, make it count, etc, and I want to do that. I like working hard.

I think most people like working hard but they don’t know what to work on. They see work harder and don’t know what that means.

It doesn’t make sense just to work harder if you don’t know what to work on or are working on the wrong thing. The other side of that phrase is work smarter, not harder.

The problem is, I see “do the work”, but what is The Work?

In trading, the initial work is studying thousands of charts. All the greats says so.

Studying charts is the hard work that others are not willing to do.

They either are not willing to do the work or they want to do the work and don’t know what the work is.

There’s so many courses and so much content to consume about trading that it’s overwhelming.

There are no shortcuts.

The hard truth of the markets is that if you take shortcuts the market will simply take all your money in return.

There is value in courses and youtube videos and books but at the end of the day it’s about putting in the work and based on what so many of the great traders say that work is studying thousands of charts. That’s why I’m collecting other peoples charts and putting them into a database. That’s why I’m spending hours a day reading and annotating charts.

Studying thousands of charts is the work and it’s where edge comes from. You build chart pattern recognition and start to develop a taste for the setups you like and don’t like. You develop your own taste and style.

I guess this is a personal manifesto of sorts.

I want to do the hard work.

I want to help people who are willing to do the work, understand what that work is.

I want to build a community that helps me stay focused and helps others do the same.

I want to work harder, do more, and keep going.
